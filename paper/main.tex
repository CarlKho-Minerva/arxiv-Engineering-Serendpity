\begin{abstract}
\todo{Write only after Sections 8--11 contain measured results.}
A behavioral clone answers ``what would I do?''; an augmenting agent must answer ``what am I failing to consider?''
We argue these are different objects, formalize the second as an exploration policy over a belief state, and test
the difference retrospectively on one subject's fifteen-year record of logged exposures and choices.
\measured{The clone half is measured: a personal LoRA raises held-out 5-way next-action prediction from 28.0\% to 37.8\% (chance 20\%; +9.8 points, 95\% CI [6.8, 13.4], gain on 20 of 21 test days).}
\proposed{The exploration half is a pre-registered retrospective ranking experiment whose falsifier is stated in Section 7.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Formalization}
Observed history $O_{1:t}$; representation $b_t \approx P(S_t \mid O_{1:t}, A_{1:t-1})$;
clone $\pi_{\mathrm{clone}}(a \mid b_t)$; augmenting policy $\pi_{\mathrm{aug}}(a \mid b_t)$.
Platform outputs $Y_t = f(z_t^{\mathrm{plat}}, C_t)$ are treated as observations from another estimator with unknown objective.
Provisional score
\begin{equation}
s(x \mid b_t) = w_r\,\mathrm{rel}(x) + w_u\,\mathrm{unc}(x) + w_n\,\mathrm{nov}(x) + w_i\,\mathrm{IG}(x) + w_o\,\mathrm{OV}(x) - w_c\,\mathrm{cost}(x),
\end{equation}
with $\mathrm{IG}(a) = H(b_t) - \mathbb{E}[H(b_{t+1}) \mid a]$. \proposed{Each term is operationalized in EXPERIMENT\_PLAN.md E2 and ablated; none is assumed well-defined.}
